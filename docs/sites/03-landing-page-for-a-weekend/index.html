Wheel & Wander: Intro Pottery

Two days of turning clay on a potter's wheel, firing your work in the kiln, and taking home at least one finished mug. Each class includes everything you need, from clay to glaze coats.

Availability

6 slots left

Book a Saturday or Sunday morning, 10:00am to 4:00pm. Each class is led by a different maker.

Clay Included

1 kg of porcelain, half a jar of stoneware, and enough glaze to cover every piece.

Firing Included

Every pot you form is fired in the electric kiln and glazed to your taste.

Tools Provided

Wheel, ribs, a sponge, and a cup for your first mug. You bring only your hands.

Beginner Friendly

Three hours at the wheel, no experience needed. The instructor is the person at the next table.
