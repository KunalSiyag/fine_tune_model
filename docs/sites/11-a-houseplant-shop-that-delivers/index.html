Adiantum

Sword fern, about 20 cm tall. Best in a pot on a north-facing windowsill.

Asplenium

Evergreen in winter, about 15 cm tall. Holds heat in the pot and stays green through December.

Nephrolepis

Spider fern, about 30 cm tall. Tolerates low light and drinks from a plate placed under the pot.

Polypodium

Rock fern, about 25 cm tall. Lives on a shelf near the kitchen window and drinks from a saucer.

Phlebodium

Orchid fern, about 25 cm tall. Drinks from a saucer and needs a few hours of morning sun.

Moss for your shelves and tables

Live moss grows on a shelf or a table for 9 months. It comes back every spring and takes about a week to water in.

Water
Once a week, by soaking the table
Light
Poor light
Soil
Peat, no pot

Hylocomas

Grey moss, about 7 cm tall. Grows on a shelf or a small table, and looks better the longer it goes without water.

Hypnum

Green moss, about 5 cm tall. Grows on a shelf for 9 months, then rests through the winter.

Bartram's

Bartram's moss, about 6 cm tall. Holds the shape of a pot through the winter and needs a little water.
