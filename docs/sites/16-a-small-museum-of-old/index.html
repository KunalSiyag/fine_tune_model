Museum of Broadcast Engineering

Turin, 1935
to now.

In 1935, Turin's first radio station began with a transmitter in the old railway yard, on Via Palermo. This museum shows the machine that made it happen and everything else it grew into.

The Collection

18 models · 1935–1952 · 240 sq metres

    • Helical shape
    • 1936

    • 40 kW

    1934 linear amplifier, class AB, used for the longest continuous play of a single record.

    • 1946
